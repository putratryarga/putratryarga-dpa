## Technical and source boundaries

1. **Brand voice and platform adaptation:** retain the brand's factual position, promise and values while adapting format, pacing, caption, hook and call-to-action to each audience/platform. Platform-native does not mean copying one asset everywhere. TikTok Creative Center and Meta Creator Marketplace are examples, not universal brand strategy standards.
2. **Editorial architecture:** a pillar describes strategic topic territory; the editorial plan operationalizes it into topics, formats, channels, owners, deadlines, approvals and publication dates. Content Marketing Institute's editorial-calendar guidance is practitioner methodology, not a mandatory standard.
3. **People-first content and source quality:** Google Search Central emphasizes audience usefulness, original value, first-hand expertise, clear sourcing and factual accuracy. It concerns Google Search and does not define every social platform's ranking or creative standards. [Google helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content?hl=id).
4. **Indonesian advertising ethics:** EPI 2020 is an industry self-regulatory code, not a statute. It distinguishes consumer testimony from endorsement; testimony should reflect real experience and not be exaggerated, while endorsement is by an individual rather than an institution/group. [EPI 2020](https://dewaniklanindonesia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/EPI-2020.pdf).
5. **Copyright and reuse:** public availability is not a licence to republish, edit or boost UGC. Check copyright, portrait/likeness, music, derivative edits, media, territory, duration and paid usage. BPK records UU 28/2014 on Copyright as in force; application to a deal requires local legal review. [BPK UU 28/2014](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38690/uu-no-28-tahun-2014).
6. **TikTok discovery and disclosure:** Creative Center exposes trend information and Top Ads examples; these are discovery signals, not representative market research or causal proof. TikTok requires Commercial Content Disclosure for promotional content. Its Commercial Music Library is the pre-cleared route for commercial music; other music requires appropriate rights. [Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-center?lang=id), [creator disclosure](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators?lang=id), [commercial music](https://support.tiktok.com/id/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok).
7. **YouTube measurement:** beginning 24 August 2026, public views are counted when playback starts across Shorts, long-form and live. Engaged views, watch time, average view duration and audience retention remain useful to interpret continued watching; raw views are not a universal cross-platform attention metric. [YouTube view-count update](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785), [engagement analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9313698).
8. **Paid promotion and partnership assets:** YouTube requires creators to declare paid placement, sponsorship and endorsement in Studio; Meta Partnership Ads permit eligible creator content to be amplified under platform authorization. Those labels do not replace copyright/usage rights or local legal disclosure duties. [YouTube paid promotion](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=id), [Meta Creator Marketplace](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/).

## High-risk distinctions

- Trend discovery is not demand validation.
- Brand voice is not identical copy; format and execution may vary while claims remain accurate.
- EPI is a self-regulatory code; the Copyright and Consumer Protection Acts have different legal status and scopes.
- Organic publishing permission, paid amplification authorization, platform partnership labels and a contract licence are separate.
- Commercial Music Library/platform media permissions are scoped and do not grant universal cross-platform rights.
- YouTube's public view count changed on 24 August 2026. Comparisons must state metric and period, and distinguish views from engaged views/retention.
- Synthetic portfolio outputs must be labelled; do not imply client work or observed market results.
