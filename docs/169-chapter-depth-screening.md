## How to interpret this screen

This is a deterministic triage check, **not** an expert score, technical-quality verdict or chapter PASS. Length and field presence do not demonstrate correctness, conceptual depth, current platform behavior, role/seniority fit, realistic Indonesia-market practice, resource fit or learner transfer. Similar-but-not-identical questions can still be conceptually repetitive. The source evidence register is an extraction/control artifact; it is not a substitute for reading and approving the canonical learner guide.

Every chapter therefore retains:
- content accuracy: OPEN;
- objective-to-resource fit: OPEN;
- independent technical/SME approval: OPEN;
- rubric calibration and learner pilot: OPEN.
