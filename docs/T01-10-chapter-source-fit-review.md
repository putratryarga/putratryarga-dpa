## Technical and access guardrails

### 1. Unit economics
OpenStax explains contribution margin and cost-volume-profit; SBA supplies a break-even calculator. These are useful finance fundamentals, but neither establishes a marketing CAC/LTV model. The curriculum still needs acquisition-cost boundaries, cohort horizon, repeat behaviour, contribution after marketing, refunds/discounts, retention and payback. Indonesian accounting/tax treatment requires qualified local review.

### 2. Research and positioning
ICC/ESOMAR is a professional ethics/accountability code, not a sampling or research-method textbook. Journey-mapping examples support qualitative synthesis but do not replace quantitative sampling, validity/reliability or Indonesian cultural/category evidence. AMA/AAMA are framing resources, not proof of tested positioning or claims.

### 3. Media planning
GovS 011 and OASIS are UK government communication-planning references and must be introduced as contextual analogies, not Indonesian law or a commercial agency standard. Reach Planner is designed for YouTube/video; access is not universal and forecasts are estimates. It is not a TV/radio/OOH cross-media currency.

### 4. Platform and credential boundaries
Google Skillshop, Meta Blueprint, TTD Edge Academy, TikTok Academy and Salesforce Trailhead teach their own platforms/ecosystems. Course completion is not equivalent to universal professional competence. Meta short paths use “Credential for Completion”; do not call this Meta Professional Certification. TikTok Academy learning content is separate from the proctored, paid Media Buying Certification exam; the latter is designed for experienced media buyers.

### 5. Measurement and privacy
GA4 now distinguishes behavioural **key events** from ad-campaign **conversions**. Consent Mode adjusts Google tags/SDK behaviour to a communicated consent state; the site/app owner remains responsible for obtaining consent and ensuring tags respect it. Vendor functionality does not establish legal compliance.

### 6. Language and learner access
HubSpot course pages reviewed did not establish English or Indonesian delivery for the specific Content Marketing/Automation routes checked. Do not count these as accessible English/Indonesian learner resources without account-side confirmation or a suitable alternative. Prices, account eligibility and learner access for every mapped resource remain OPEN.
