## Method and evidence rules

This matrix transcribes and updates the five-agency role-evidence statement attached to the first chapter of each track in `docs/169-chapter-evidence-register.md`. The evidence is role-level, not chapter-specific. Each chapter inherits its track's role-level note; that does not constitute 169 independent JD validations.

Evidence classes: **A** direct employer-hosted job description; **B** adjacent role evidence; **C** agency capability/service evidence; **D** employer career-index/title, historical or syndicated evidence; **G** no sufficiently specific evidence located in the audited source batch. Compound codes such as A/D mean mixed evidence. A class is not a score and does not prove every competency in the track. Geography, seniority, publication date/currentness and exact role scope must remain attached to each source.

## 4. Five taxonomy boundaries tested

- **Full-Stack vs Performance Marketing:** integrated generalist learning path versus cross-channel paid-growth role. A multi-channel paid-media operator is not proof of full-stack scope.
- **Organic Social vs Content vs Community:** Organic Social remains a specialization within Track 13; Track 16 owns member relationships, participation, moderation, care, safety and governance; Paid Social remains Track 5.
- **Media Analytics vs Marketing Science:** Track 10 retains descriptive/diagnostic analytics and causal/experimental/model-based pathways. Reporting/BI JDs do not by themselves validate MMM or incrementality.
- **Paid Search vs Google Ads suite:** Track 4 is search-intent, query/keyword, negatives, bidding, search ads and search measurement. Cross-channel Google Ads exposure does not prove pure SEM scope; DV360 is not Paid Search.
- **Direct publisher vs Programmatic vs Traditional:** Track 8 owns direct publisher/media-owner relationships and IO/commercial negotiation; Track 6 owns DSP trading; Traditional Media Planning & Buying remains Elective 18.

## 5. Cell-specific cautions and gaps

- **Track 2:** WPP Jakarta and Dentsu Jakarta have direct connection-planning JDs; Publicis and other cells retain actual evidence classes and should not be assumed equivalent.
- **Track 3:** Publicis Jakarta and Dentsu Jakarta planning roles support local planning competencies; an integrated digital planner is not automatically a traditional buyer or connection strategist.
- **Track 4:** WPP's Search career-index roles and cross-channel Search execution evidence do not remove the direct SEM gap in Dentsu or prove identical scope across groups.
- **Track 5:** WPP and Havas now have employer-hosted paid-social role evidence. Keep paid activation distinct from organic publishing/community.
- **Track 6:** Dentsu now has a direct Programmatic Specialist JD (Lisbon); preserve its execution/support seniority and geography.
- **Track 7:** Marketplace operations, retail media network activation and affiliate commission partnerships are distinct. Require exact responsibility evidence.
- **Track 8:** Direct publisher negotiation and IO ownership are not the same as DSP trading or offline media buying.
- **Track 9:** WPP and Publicis direct JDs support trafficking, tags, pixels, UTMs, QA and go-live controls.
- **Track 10:** Reporting/BI roles support descriptive and diagnostic analytics; causal inference, incrementality, MMM and experimentation require separate method-level evidence.
- **Track 11:** WPP and Dentsu now have direct SEO JDs; AEO/GEO is emerging and does not guarantee AI visibility. Havas direct SEO role evidence remains open in this batch.
- **Track 12:** CRM campaign operations, automation execution and CRM Director strategy are different seniority/scope levels. WPP and Omnicom direct CRM JD evidence remains open in this batch.
- **Track 13:** Content planning, organic social publishing, social strategy and community operations overlap but are not synonyms.
- **Track 14:** Influencer strategy, talent/creator management, paid partnership ads and content production are related but distinct responsibilities. Dentsu/Havas direct creator-partnership JD evidence remains open in this batch.
- **Track 15:** WPP now has employer-hosted affiliate evidence including a Jakarta leadership listing and a detailed Beirut technical JD. Affiliate is not interchangeable with retail media or all forms of social commerce.
- **Track 16:** Community management requires member relationships, participation, moderation, care, safety and governance; social publishing alone is insufficient. Havas direct community JD evidence remains open.
