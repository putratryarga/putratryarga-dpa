# T07 — Retail & Commerce Media: 10-Chapter Source-Fit Review
**Review date:** 5 November 2026  
**Scope:** DPA-T07-CH01 through DPA-T07-CH10, current chapter objectives, local platform documentation and retail-media measurement guidance.  
**Disposition:** FIRST-PASS DESK REVIEW COMPLETE; 10/10 CHAPTERS PARTIAL; FORMAL ACCEPTANCE OPEN.

## Executive finding

T07's original source set relied heavily on Walmart Connect and Amazon Ads Academy, with supporting Google Merchant Center, TikTok Shop and Lazada Seller Center routes. These sources are not interchangeable, and Walmart/Amazon features are not direct proxies for Indonesia's marketplace products.

The new local-source pass adds current platform help for TikTok Shop/Product Shopping Ads/GMV Max, Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads, Shopee Ads creation and reporting, and Lazada Sponsored Solutions. These sources make the curriculum more locally grounded and reveal major eligibility and measurement differences.

All ten chapters remain PARTIAL. This desk review is not approval of current seller-account access, commercial terms, product availability, measurement integrity or chapter accuracy. Platform features can be conditional on account type, seller status, integration, permissions, campaign migration and market.

## What the local platform sources establish

### 1. TikTok Shop Ads and GMV Max

TikTok's Indonesian Product Shopping Ads help page was last updated March 2026. It says GMV Max became the default and only supported campaign type for TikTok Shop Ads from July 2025. Product Shopping Ads use product images/details from a Product Detail Page; available in Indonesia; ads for out-of-stock products pause; current Seller Center product information is used. Optimization objectives include gross revenue/target ROAS, purchases/target CPA and clicks/CPC. The page notes eligible Indonesian sellers may choose Tokopedia placement, with a minimum daily budget of IDR 6,000 and up to 300 specific products for that placement.

Important nuance: the same help page separately states a normal specific-product limit of up to 50 products, while its Tokopedia placement note gives up to 300. Do not collapse these into one universal product limit; identify the placement and campaign context.

Sources: [TikTok — Product Shopping Ads Indonesia (Mar 2026)](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-product-shopping-ads?lang=id) and [Product GMV Max campaign guidance (June 2026)](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-create-a-product-gmv-max-campaign-in-tiktok-ads-manager).

### 2. Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads

TikTok's March 2026 Tokopedia guidance states that Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads is only available to invited sellers. With the Tokopedia integration, ads can appear in Tokopedia Search and Recommendation placements and are managed in TikTok Ads Manager. Campaigns created in TikTok Ads Manager report there, while campaigns created in Tokopedia Seller Center retain separate reporting there. Integrating Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads deactivates the old Tokopedia advertising platform for campaign management.

This is a specific eligibility/integration route, not evidence that every Tokopedia seller has access or that all historical reporting is unified.

Source: [TikTok — About Product Shopping Ads on Tokopedia (Mar 2026)](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-product-shopping-ads-on-tokopedia?lang=id).

### 3. Shopee Ads

Shopee Ads' Indonesian reporting guide defines impressions, clicks, CTR, orders, conversions, products sold, sales, expenses, ROAS, ACOS and conversion rate. The guide says multiple outcome metrics use a 7-day period after an ad click; sales can include products other than the advertised product in the seller's store; the last-click ad can receive conversion credit. These rules materially affect comparisons with another platform's attribution and SKU/order basis.

Shopee's product-ad guide also describes automatic, group and individual product selection, with up to 30 selected products in some ad group workflows.

Sources: [Shopee Ads — Monitor Ad Performance](https://iklan.shopee.co.id/learn/faq/555/8) and [Shopee Ads — Create Product Ads](https://iklan.shopee.co.id/learn/faq/574/1716).

### 4. Lazada Sponsored Solutions

Lazada Indonesia's Sponsored Solutions FAQ identifies a broader product portfolio: Promosi Max, Mega Sales Accelerator, Promosi Produk, Promosi Display, Sponsored Brand Search, affiliate, direct matching, external traffic and media promotion. It includes help topics for Sponsored Discovery, audience bidding, keyword selection, product selection, attribution windows, reporting/export, CTR/CPC/ROI and feature access. The FAQ marks some products such as display/media/external traffic as seller-whitelist-only; other features have different eligibility.

The Lazada FAQ is a useful official product route, but it is not a full audited feature comparison or measurement standard. Seller eligibility, costs, billing and available features must be checked for the actual seller account.

Sources: [Lazada Sponsored Solutions Indonesia — FAQ](https://www.lazadasolutions.com/id/id-faqs/) and [Sponsored Discovery campaign management](https://www.lazadasolutions.com/id/faq/bagaimana-cara-mengelola-campaign-sponsored-discovery-di-aplikasi-lazada-seller-center/).

## Measurement standards and limits

### IAB/MRC Retail Media Measurement Guidelines (January 2024)

The U.S.-oriented guideline includes data collection and quality control, audience metrics, ad delivery/viewability/IVT, attribution windows, sales/outcome metrics, halo/assisted outcomes and incrementality. It emphasizes disclosure of methodology, attributed sales, online/offline distinctions and incrementality design. It is an important reference for measurement discipline, but it does not prove any Indonesian marketplace implements or is audited against every recommendation.

Source: [IAB/MRC Retail Media Measurement Guidelines (PDF)](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/IAB_Retail_Media_Measurement_Guidelines_January2024.pdf).

### IAB Europe Commerce Media Measurement Standards V2.1 (22 January 2026)

V2.1 introduces a refined measurement funnel, standardized gross/net sales, quick-commerce metrics, incrementality methodologies, new-to-brand/new-to-category timeframes and measurement-tool guidance. Its page describes a default 30-day reporting lookback with customizable retailer/ad-tech options and a six-month transition period ending July 2026 for the European certification programme.

This is European industry guidance. The end of its transition period does not establish Indonesian marketplace adoption or identical attribution windows across local platforms.

Source: [IAB Europe — Commerce Media Measurement Standards V2.1](https://iabeurope.eu/knowledge_hub/iab-europes-commerce-incl-retail-media-measurement-standards-v2/).

## Critical curriculum guardrails

1. **Do not call every sponsored marketplace placement a fully mature retail media network.** Describe the platform's actual product, data/measurement offer and seller eligibility.
2. **Do not treat ROAS/ACOS as comparable across marketplaces without reconciling the attributed sales basis, click/view window, product/SKU scope, voucher/discount treatment, returns/cancellations, currency and last-click logic.**
3. **Do not label platform-attributed sales as incremental sales.** The IAB/MRC guideline defines incrementality as causal impact above a baseline and recommends methodology/data-integrity transparency; platform attribution alone does not establish it.
4. **Do not generalize Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads to all sellers.** The official route is invited/eligible seller only; integration changes the campaign-management/reporting path.
5. **Do not assume TikTok Shop Ads remains an old manual campaign-type stack.** Current docs identify GMV Max as the default/only supported TikTok Shop Ads campaign type since July 2025; product-level controls and limits depend on placement and account.
6. **Do not assume every Lazada seller can activate every paid solution.** The official FAQ marks some options as whitelist-only.
