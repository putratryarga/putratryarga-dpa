# T07 — Retail & Commerce Media: 10-Chapter Source-Fit Review

**Review date:** 5 November 2026  
**Disposition:** First-pass desk review complete; all ten chapters PARTIAL. Formal objective acceptance, local access/eligibility and SME approval remain OPEN.

## Executive finding

The prior T07 source set leaned on Walmart Connect and Amazon Ads Academy. Those are learning routes, not direct proxies for Indonesian marketplace ad products. The mapping now adds current Indonesia-facing documentation from TikTok Shop Ads/GMV Max, Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads, Shopee Ads and Lazada Sponsored Solutions.

These sources establish specific platform workflows and constraints, not a unified retail-media marketplace model. They differ in seller eligibility, product types, reporting ownership, optimization objectives, product-selection limits and sales-attribution rules. All ten T07 chapters remain PARTIAL because the current material still lacks neutral local comparison, commercial terms, access evidence and qualified measurement review.

## Local platform evidence

### TikTok Shop Ads / GMV Max

TikTok's Indonesian Product Shopping Ads help page was last updated March 2026. It says GMV Max became the default and only supported campaign type for TikTok Shop Ads from July 2025. Product Shopping Ads use product images/details from a Product Detail Page; it is available in Indonesia; out-of-stock items pause; and optimization options include gross revenue/target ROAS, purchases/target CPA and clicks/CPC. The page separately notes a normal specific-product limit and a different limit for eligible Tokopedia placement, so these must not be generalized into one universal cap.

Sources: [TikTok Product Shopping Ads Indonesia](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-product-shopping-ads?lang=id), [Product GMV Max campaign guidance](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-create-a-product-gmv-max-campaign-in-tiktok-ads-manager).

### Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads

TikTok's March 2026 guidance describes Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads as invite/eligibility-gated. Integrated campaigns can be managed through TikTok Ads Manager, while campaigns created in Tokopedia Seller Center retain separate reporting there. Integration changes the campaign-management pathway and can deactivate the former Tokopedia ad-management route.

Source: [TikTok Product Shopping Ads on Tokopedia](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-product-shopping-ads-on-tokopedia?lang=id).

### Shopee Ads

Shopee's Indonesian help defines platform-specific metrics including impressions, clicks, CTR, orders, conversions, products sold, sales, expense, ROAS, ACOS and conversion rate. Its reporting guide uses a 7-day post-click window for several sales outcomes; attributed outcomes can include other products from the seller's store, and the last-click ad may receive credit. Product-ad setup describes automatic, group and individual product-selection options.

Sources: [Shopee Ads — Monitor Ad Performance](https://iklan.shopee.co.id/learn/faq/555/8), [Shopee Ads — Create Product Ads](https://iklan.shopee.co.id/learn/faq/574/1716).

### Lazada Sponsored Solutions

Lazada's Indonesian FAQ lists onsite solutions (Promosi Max, Promosi Produk, Promosi Display, Sponsored Brand Search) and offsite options (affiliate, direct matching, external traffic and media promotion). It includes help for audience bidding, keyword/product selection, attribution window, performance reports and seller feature eligibility. Some products are marked as whitelist-only.

Sources: [Lazada Sponsored Solutions Indonesia FAQ](https://www.lazadasolutions.com/id/id-faqs/), [Sponsored Discovery campaign management](https://www.lazadasolutions.com/id/faq/bagaimana-cara-mengelola-campaign-sponsored-discovery-di-aplikasi-lazada-seller-center/).

## Measurement references and limitations

**IAB/MRC Retail Media Measurement Guidelines (January 2024).** The document covers data collection/quality, audience measurement, viewability/IVT, attribution windows, outcome metrics, halo/assisted outcomes and incrementality. It defines incrementality as causal impact above baseline and calls for transparency around attribution methodology, time windows, online/offline sales and extrapolation. It is a US-oriented industry guideline; its presence does not prove Indonesian marketplace implementation or audit compliance.

Source: [IAB/MRC Retail Media Measurement Guidelines](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/IAB_Retail_Media_Measurement_Guidelines_January2024.pdf).

**IAB Europe Commerce Media Measurement Standards V2.1 (22 January 2026).** V2.1 refines the measurement funnel, standardizes gross/net sales definitions, adds quick-commerce metrics and incrementality methodologies, and updates new-to-brand/new-to-category timeframes. Its article describes a default 30-day lookback with configurable options and a six-month Europe certification transition through July 2026. This does not establish Indonesian marketplace adoption or shared attribution windows.

Source: [IAB Europe Commerce Media Measurement Standards V2.1](https://iabeurope.eu/knowledge_hub/iab-europes-commerce-incl-retail-media-measurement-standards-v2/).

## Critical curriculum guardrails

1. Do not call every marketplace-sponsored placement a complete retail media network. Describe the actual product, data/measurement offer and eligibility.
2. Do not compare ROAS/ACOS across marketplaces before reconciling sales basis, click/view window, product/SKU scope, discounts/vouchers, returns/cancellations, currency and last-click logic.
3. Do not label platform-attributed sales as incremental sales. Attribution is not a causal estimate.
4. Do not generalize Tokopedia Product Shopping Ads access to all sellers; the current help route describes invitation/eligibility constraints and a changed management/reporting path.
5. Do not assume all Lazada sponsored features are available to every seller; some are whitelist-gated.
6. Do not assume manual campaign controls remain the same across TikTok Shop Ads after GMV Max became the default/only supported campaign type.
