## 1. Merger status verdict (verified)

- **CONFIRMED — Omnicom completed its acquisition of The Interpublic Group of Companies on November 26, 2025.** Official press release: "Omnicom (NYSE: OMC) today announced the successful completion of its acquisition of The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc." (omc.com newsroom, Nov 26, 2025; also the Nov 26, 2025 exchange-offer results release: "its merger with The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. ('IPG') closed on November 26, 2025"). Deal was announced Dec 8–9, 2024 (merger agreement EX-2.1 dated Dec 8, 2024, SEC). FTC final order Sept 26, 2025 with compliance monitor. Legacy Omnicom shareholders ~60.6% / legacy IPG ~39.4%; pro forma revenue >$25B; ticker OMC. Confidence: **high** (primary source).
- **IPG is now part of Omnicom; IPG Mediabrands as a structure is retired.** IPG Mediabrands was folded into the combined company's media division **"Omnicom Media"** (Adweek, Dec 2, 2025 — also reports IPG Mediabrands CEO Eileen Kiernan out; Storyboard18, first published Dec 1, 2025, updated Apr 10, 2026). Omnicom Media now houses **six media agency brands: OMD, PHD, Hearts & Science, UM, Initiative, Mediahub**, alongside **Acxiom** (data/identity), led globally by Florian Adamski. UM and Initiative continue operating but solely as part of Omnicom Media. Confidence: **high–medium** (two trade sources + consistent with ATS "Agency:" metadata).
- **Creative networks:** Omnicom Advertising aligns **BBDO, McCann and TBWA** as its three global networks (omc.com "Omnicom Advertising Announces Leadership Transition"; Ad Age, Campaign, Adweek — all Dec 1, 2025). **DDB, FCB and MullenLowe are being phased out in H1 2026** (FCB→BBDO; DDB and MullenLowe→TBWA), with ~4,000 roles cut. **Implication for this matrix: DDB is no longer a go-to-market network as of the 2026 restructure; BBDO/TBWA/OMD/PHD/Hearts & Science/FleishmanHillard/Ketchum (legacy Omnicom) and UM/Initiative/Mediahub (legacy IPG) sit inside Omnicom.** Confidence: **high** (four concordant trade sources + official omc.com page).
- **Careers infrastructure:** omnicommedia.com/careers (market selector) routes into the shared OMC Workday ATS; ATS job metadata carries an "Agency:" field (e.g., "Agency: Omnicom Media", "Agency: OMD", "Agency: Omnicom Precision Marketing"), enabling BU attribution per posting.

## 4. Narrative

### Structure summary (incl. IPG merger impact)
Omnicom closed the IPG acquisition on 2025-11-26 (omc.com, primary source) and is now a single >$25B-revenue group trading as OMC. For this matrix the consequences are: (1) the evidence base for the "Omnicom" column is now the shared OMC Workday ATS (hosted on the legacy IPG Workday tenant, `interpublic.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/OMC`) — one employer surface covering OMD, PHD, Hearts & Science, UM, Initiative, Mediahub, Acxiom, the creative networks and specialist units; (2) IPG Mediabrands no longer exists as a separate structure — its networks continue as brands inside Omnicom Media, so legacy-IPG evidence (e.g., the prior matrix's Omnicom sources already sitting on this tenant) is properly Omnicom evidence today; (3) creative-side consolidation matters for content/community tracks: BBDO, McCann, TBWA are the go-to-market networks, and DDB/FCB/MullenLowe are being retired in H1 2026 — curriculum copy should not cite "DDB" as a current Omnicom network; (4) the ATS "Agency:" metadata field preserves per-BU attribution, which this audit used (e.g., OMD UK AdOps, MG OMD Affiliates).

### Terminology differences (vs. the 16 track names)
- **"Outcomes"** (Outcomes Planning & Buying Manager, Outcomes Associate Director) is the legacy-IPG umbrella for biddable activation across Search/Social/Programmatic — maps to T04–T06 as a family, not to a single track.
- **"Performance Media"** is the ATS job-subfunction for paid search (T04); "Performance" also titles planning roles (T01) — same word, two scopes.
- **"Commerce"** is the preferred title stem for T07 (Commerce Specialist, Manager, Commerce) with "Retail Media"/"eCommerce" appearing inside titles; "Retail DSP" as a title was not found.
- **"Investment"** = the buying/investment family (T08/E-TM); traditional TV surfaces as "National Video," OOH and "audio-vidéo" (Canada-FR), not as "Traditional Media."
- **"Connection(s) Planning"** appears with both spellings (T02); "GEO" is explicit in SEO director titles (T11) — direct AEO/GEO terminology adoption.
- **"Creator Activation"** is an OMG managed-service product name (T14); community work hides inside combined "Social Media and Community Manager" titles (T16).

### Top gaps
1. **E-CRO is the only D cell:** the direct CRO evidence is LinkedIn-hosted (Omnicom Media Nederland) and no CRO/conversion/experimentation posting exists on the official OMC ATS as of 2026-11-04; no official CRO capability page was located either (no C-class source). Fix: monitor the ATS for "CRO"/"Experimentation" postings; capture the NL JD full text while a mirror is fresh.
2. **Geography skew:** every 2026 posting found is US/UK/Canada/EU. No Indonesia/SEA-market Omnicom posting was located in this pass — consistent with the prior doc's caution against transferring market evidence to Indonesia; SEA-specific cells (e.g., Jakarta) remain unevidenced.
3. **Full-text archiving:** Workday's JS rendering blocked direct JD retrieval; all responsibility quotes are search-index snippets of employer pages. Competency-level extraction per track (prior doc's "Remaining validation work" item 2) still requires the full JD texts.
4. **Social-commerce sub-type (T15):** affiliate is now well evidenced, but "social commerce" as a distinct mandate (TikTok Shop, live commerce) was not found in any title/JD snippet.
