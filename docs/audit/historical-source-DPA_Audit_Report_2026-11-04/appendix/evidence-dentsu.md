## 1. Structure summary (verified 2026-11-04)

- **Two operational networks inside Dentsu Group Inc.** (pure holding company since 1 Jan 2020): *dentsu Japan network* and *dentsu international* (HQ London). This is the boilerplate printed on current Job Descriptions (e.g., Sr. Connection Planner R1131719, retrieved 2026-11-04): "the Dentsu Group encompasses two operational networks: dentsu Japan network… and dentsu international… through its five leadership brands—Carat, dentsu X, iProspect, Dentsu Creative, and Merkle." (Workday, fetched 2026-11-04.)
- **Brand list — what is current.** dentsu.com APAC "Our network" (accessed 2026-11-04) shows: **Carat, Dentsu B2B, dentsu creative, dentsu X, iProspect, Merkle, tag**. Ad Age datacenter profile (16 Jun 2026): "Dentsu Group went to market with six key agency brands: Carat, Dentsu Creative, Dentsu X, iProspect, Merkle and Tag." dentsu Indonesia site (accessed 2026-11-04) lists: **iProspect, dentsu X, Carat, Dentsu Creative, Merkle, Tag + local Dwi Sapta dentsu (DWI)**.
- **"dentsu ONE" is NOT a current global brand.** "One dentsu" was the 2019/2020 rebrand strategy of Dentsu Aegis Network → dentsu (Wikipedia, Dentsu International; dentsu news release 28 Sep 2020). "dentsu One" survives only as legacy local creative-agency names (dentsu One Taipei per dentsu.com/tw; Dentsu One Bangkok per brandinginasia.com; Dentsu One Redder, Vietnam per LinkedIn), and dentsu itself warns of scams "using the name of 'dentsu One'" (dentsu.com/see/en/our-news). The prior claim of consolidation "into dentsu ONE" is unsupported.
- **Amplifi is still alive** as dentsu's media investment arm: live agency pages (dentsu.com/hu/en/who-we-are/our-agencies/amplifi), a current requisition "Senior Director, Amplifi Enablement" (Göteborg, R1133018, on Workday list 2026-11-04), and two live JDs carry `Brand: Amplifi` (Affiliates Director R1132169; Media Planner Jakarta R1128963). But Amplifi is not in the JD-boilerplate "five leadership brands" and not on the APAC/ID homepage logo strips.
- **Hiring data uses many brand fields**, confirming multi-brand operations persist in practice: workday requisitions on 2026-11-04 carry Brand = Dentsu X, iProspect, Amplifi, Dentsu Media, Dentsu Creative, Merkle, Carat, Happy Marketer (a legacy SEA CRM consultancy), Tag.
- **2026 restructuring is real and deep.** Mid-Term Management Plan Update FY2026–2028 (group.dentsu.com/en/about-us/mtmp.html); Campaign (17 Aug 2026): Dentsu considering cutting up to 160 overseas entities by 2028; exchange4media/MMM/Marketing-Interactive (18 Aug 2026): 70–80 international entities to be cut in FY2026, ~900 international jobs eliminated in H1 2026, ¥26bn restructuring spend planned for 2026; mi-3 (8 May 2026): in Australia, Carat and iProspect brands retired into a single unified local media agency; Mitchellake intelligence (Oct 2026): 360i reintroduced as a specialist social team inside Dentsu Creative; FT/truwater advisory (Sep 2026): reported exploration of divesting the international business. None of this changed brand-facing hiring during our audit window — 799 roles were live on the global Workday on 2026-11-04.
- **Indonesia (dentsu Indonesia).** dentsu.com/id/en is live ("Integrated Marketing Network & Agency Services | dentsu Indonesia"), with Creative/CX/Media case studies (Blibli.com, Ruangguru). The global Workday carried **exactly 7 live Jakarta requisitions on 2026-11-04** (search `q=Jakarta` = "7 JOBS FOUND"): Copywriter (Dentsu Creative), Art Director (Dentsu Creative), Account Director (Dentsu Creative), Account Manager (Dentsu Creative, posted 27 Oct 2026), Associate Business Director (Dentsu Creative, posted ~28 Oct 2026), Media Planner (Amplifi, OOH scope, posted ~late Sep 2026), Sr. Connection Planner (Dentsu X, posted ~early Oct 2026). **No live Jakarta vacancies in performance, programmatic, SEO, CRM or analytics on this date** — an Indonesia-specific demand gap for those tracks.

### Terminology differences vs the DPA curriculum
- Dentsu hires **"Connection Planner"** (our T02), not "Communications Planner".
- Biddable work is titled **Performance Manager / Integrated Performance Manager / Paid Search / Paid Social / Programmatic Buyer**; the prior doc's "Digital Consultant – Biddable" (R1123158) could not be re-verified live and dedicated paid-search/paid-social titles now coexist with cross-channel biddable roles.
- "Media Planner" is confirmed live — but the current Jakarta posting is **OOH-specialised under Amplifi**, not the integrated ATL/BTL/digital planner the prior doc described.
- Retail/commerce media sits in a **"Commerce & Retail Media" team; "New Stream Media"** appears in the unit name of current requisitions (R1136080/81); affiliate is a distinct **Affiliates & Lead Generation team (Amplifi, Manchester)**.
- CRM sits under **Merkle / Happy Marketer (SEA)**; SEO is being repositioned as **"Total Search" with LLMO / AI Discovery / GEO** language (iProspect/dentsu Media US; Merkle).
