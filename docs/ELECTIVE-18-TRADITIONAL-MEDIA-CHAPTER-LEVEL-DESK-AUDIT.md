## Technical and local-market boundaries

- Nielsen Indonesia's public explainer describes panel and big-data inputs for TV audience measurement, but is not a current local trading currency or ratecard: [TV audience measurement](https://www.nielsen.com/id/insights/2023/how-to-measure-tv-audiences/).
- The MRC December 2025 OOH standard distinguishes traffic, gross impressions, viewable/OTS, likelihood-to-see and audience; not an Indonesian audience currency: [MRC OOH Standards](https://mediaratingcouncil.org/sites/default/files/Standards/MRC%20OOH%20Standards%20Combined_FINAL.pdf).
- MRC Digital Audio covers digital-audio measurement and must not be treated as terrestrial AM/FM audience currency: [MRC Digital Audio Standards](https://mediaratingcouncil.org/sites/default/files/Standards/Digital%20Audio%20Measurement%20Standards%20-%20Final%20Version%201.0.pdf).
- Cross-media reach cannot be summed without compatible universes, periods and deduplication. BPS population counts are not media audience measurement.
- Digital insertion-order terms do not automatically govern offline bookings or Indonesian contracts; authorized legal/finance review remains required.
