## Important distinctions

1. A vendor course completion credential, learning-path award, professional certification and award/competition win are different evidence types.
2. A course page describing a “certificate” does not automatically make the credential equivalent to a proctored professional certification.
3. A free course does not imply a free professional exam.
4. Regional prices can differ materially. US voucher prices must not be copied into an Indonesia learning roadmap.
5. Platform credentials validate platform-specific knowledge. They do not replace cross-platform strategy, measurement, commercial judgement or portfolio evidence.
6. A portfolio should contain a realistic build, decision rationale, QA evidence, results from clearly labelled synthetic data, limitations and reflection. A badge alone is not a work sample.
