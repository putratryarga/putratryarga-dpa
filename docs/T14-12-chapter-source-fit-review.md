## 2. Critical Indonesian regulatory update

**Permendag 19/2026 is the current PMSE instrument identified in this review.** JDIH BPK records it as effective on 8 June 2026 and states that it revokes Permendag 31/2023. Use the official Kemendag PDF for clause-level review:
- [JDIH BPK — Permendag 19/2026](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/351720/permendag-no-19-tahun-2026)
- [Official Kemendag PDF — Permendag 19/2026](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/pdf/Regulasi/2026/PERMENDAG%2019%20TAHUN%202026.pdf)

Relevant provisions:
- **Articles 27–29:** making and distribution of electronic advertising for marketing/promotion, including publication and dissemination through electronic communications.
- **Article 30:** where electronic advertising is delivered through PPMSE facilities, the PPMSE must comply with applicable broadcasting, privacy/personal-data, consumer-protection and competition requirements.
- **Article 31:** business actors who make, provide facilities for, and/or disseminate electronic advertising must ensure the material does not conflict with law and are responsible for the ad material.
- **Article 32(1):** electronic advertising must not mislead consumers about product/service quality, quantity, ingredients, use, price, warranty or delivery; it must not contain incorrect information, must provide relevant use-risk information, must not exploit a person/event without permission or consent, and must provide a clear close/skip control.
- **Article 32(2):** electronic advertising that shows reviews/testimonials from consumers who have used the goods/services must include or hold and ensure the truth of relevant legal-subject identity information and be conducted responsibly.

**Scope caution:** this is a PMSE/electronic-advertising instrument. Do not extend a clause beyond its statutory scope without qualified advice about the transaction, actor and communication context. BPK metadata confirms status/commencement; use the official PDF for clause-level interpretation.

### EPI 2020 — testimony versus endorsement

EPI 2020 is an industry self-regulatory code, **not a statute**:
- Clause 1.16.1: testimony is made in an individual's own name, not for an institution/group/community.
- Clause 1.16.2: testimony must describe an event genuinely experienced and not exaggerated.
- Clause 1.16.3: where a claimed benefit requires regular or time-specific use, the experience must meet those conditions.
- Clause 1.16.4: consumer testimony must be supportable by a written statement signed by the consumer.
- Clause 1.16.5: identity/address details must be producible to the advertising ethics enforcement body if requested.
- Clause 1.17: endorsement is given by an individual, not on behalf of an institution/group/community.

Source: [Dewan Periklanan Indonesia — EPI 2020 PDF](https://p3i-pusat.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Etika-Pariwara-Indonesia-versi-2020.pdf). Do not collapse 1.16 (consumer testimony) into 1.17 (endorsement), and do not present EPI as legislation. Where identity/address records are processed, separately assess purpose, legal basis, minimization, access, retention and security.

## 3. Supporting source scope

- [UU 8/1999 — Consumer Protection](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999), [UU 27/2022 — Personal Data Protection](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022) and [UU 28/2014 — Copyright](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38690/uu-no-28-tahun-2014) are recorded as in force on BPK records at the time of the check. Status does not settle clause-by-clause applicability.
- [ANA Influencer Marketing Measurement Guidelines](https://www.ana.net/miccontent/show/id/rr-2022-06-ana-influencer-marketing-measurement) provide awareness, engagement and conversion families and note that platforms calculate engagement rates/video views differently. They do not prove causal incrementality or an Indonesian measurement currency.
- [IAB — Creator Economy As-Is Measurement Landscape (29 Jan 2026)](https://www.iab.com/guidelines/creator-economy-as-is-measurement-landscape/) identifies fragmented metrics, platform silos, proxy-based ROI and missing standards/currencies/financial rigor. It is a market landscape report, not a settled standard.
- [TikTok One Project Reporting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-tiktok-one-campaign-reporting?lang=id) defines TikTok-specific organic versus paid views/engagement and project/video metrics; its formulas and regional report availability must not be generalized.
- [TikTok Commercial Content Disclosure](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators?lang=en) and [YouTube paid-promotion disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=id) describe platform-specific settings, not the entirety of local legal/contract duties.
- [Meta Creator Marketplace / Partnership Ads](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/) and [Meta Blueprint Partnership Ads training (Apr 2026)](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/activity/707398) describe Meta's own discovery and ad activation; technical permission does not grant copyright, likeness, music or broader contract rights.
- [TikTok One creator collaboration](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-working-with-creators-on-tiktok-one-campaigns?lang=en) describes project invitations, collaboration and Ads Manager sync; confirm region/account access before treating a feature as learner-available.
- [YouTube Fake Engagement Policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767?hl=en-419) is a YouTube policy, not a universal cross-platform fraud-screening standard.
