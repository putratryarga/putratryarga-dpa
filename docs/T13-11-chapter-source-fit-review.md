# T13 — Content Strategy & Creation: 11-Chapter Source-Fit Review

**Date:** 5 November 2026  
**Disposition:** First-pass desk review complete; all 11 chapters PARTIAL. Formal objective fit, local access, rights and SME approval remain OPEN.

## Executive finding

The source map now includes TikTok Creative Center/Top Ads, TikTok commercial-music and creator-disclosure help, YouTube Analytics metric definitions, W3C WCAG 2.2, Press Council media guidance and Indonesia's copyright-law record. This improves platform-specific and governance coverage but does not complete the content strategy/editorial curriculum.

Platform data has distinct scope. TikTok Top Ads is an ad-inspiration and ad-performance resource, not a representative sample of organic posts or consumer culture. YouTube impressions/CTR/retention are YouTube-native definitions, not a common cross-platform content currency.

## Source scope and limitations

**TikTok Creative Center.** The public Creative Center was last updated July 2026 and includes trends, Top Ads, keyword insights, ad examples and creative tools. Top Ads supports inspiration and comparison of creative examples, but does not prove organic-content effectiveness or causal lift.

**Commercial music and creator disclosure.** TikTok recommends the Commercial Music Library for commercial promotional posts because its other music licences do not automatically cover commercial use. Its creator disclosure guide, updated April 2026, requires commercial-content disclosure when posting material that promotes a brand, product or service. Platform settings do not replace a separate review of music, talent, stock, creator and territory rights or applicable local requirements.

**YouTube Analytics.** Official help distinguishes Reach and Engagement reporting. It defines audience-retention reports, watch time and average view duration. Thumbnail impressions have a platform-specific threshold (more than one second and at least 50% visible); external sites/apps are not counted in the same way. The Content tab recommends comparing like formats because viewer behavior differs across Videos, Shorts, Live and Posts.

**Indonesian copyright law.** JDIH BPK records UU No. 28 Tahun 2014 about Copyright as effective in its current record. However, the amendment bill remains under legislative discussion: DPR reported the Panja formation on 6 October 2026 and further Panja discussion/public input on 27 October 2026. The draft is not the same as an enacted statute; legal status and applicable implementing rules must be rechecked before teaching detailed clauses or giving legal advice.

## Required remediation

1. Maintain a rights register per asset: creator/owner, music and image source, licence, territory, term, media, paid/organic usage, edit rights, creator usage/whitelisting, expiry and proof.
2. Keep separate native metric dictionaries; do not treat TikTok Top Ads paid creative performance as organic content results or YouTube impressions as equivalent to other platform metrics.
3. Add independent long-form editorial, source verification, corrections and accessibility materials.
4. Validate Instagram and LinkedIn platform-native formats, analytics and branded-content rules through current official documentation and account access.
5. Require Indonesian legal review of copyright/royalty updates and editorial review of disclosure/creator agreements. The RUU Copyright process is still active in the latest DPR material found in this pass.
