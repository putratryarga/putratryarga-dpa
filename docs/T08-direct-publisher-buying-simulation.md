# T08 Direct Publisher Buying Simulation Pack

**Status:** Tier 3 applied learning simulation — synthetic, non-client, non-market-benchmark data  
**Track:** T08 — Media Investment, Trading & Publisher Partnerships  
**Chapters:** DPA-T08-CH01 to DPA-T08-CH10  
**Version:** 0.1 · 5 November 2026

> **Important:** Every publisher, proposal, rate, volume, score, date and commercial assumption in this exercise is fictional and is labelled **SYNTHETIC**. It is not a real Indonesian media-owner rate card, market benchmark, quotation, signed insertion order, legal instrument or tax opinion. Use current publisher-issued media kits, proposals, booking orders, invoices and authorized legal/procurement/tax review for real transactions.

## 1. Learning purpose and boundaries

This simulation teaches the direct publisher/media-owner buying lifecycle: source and qualify inventory, request proposals, normalize commercial offers, negotiate value and terms, confirm the booking, monitor delivery, resolve shortfalls, reconcile invoices and evaluate the partner.

T08 is not:
- T03 Media Planning (channel mix, audience strategy, reach/frequency planning and forecast);
- T06 Programmatic Trading (DSP/SSP, RTB, auction and automated deal activation);
- T09 Ad Operations (technical trafficking, tags, pixels, campaign setup and technical QA); or
- Elective 18 Traditional Media Planning & Buying (offline TV, radio, print, OOH/DOOH, cinema and sponsorship planning/buying).

T08 owns the commercial buying and publisher-partnership workflow. It must coordinate with planners, Ad Ops, finance, procurement, legal and client teams without silently taking over their approval authority.

## 3. Synthetic publisher proposals

The three suppliers below are invented solely for the exercise.

| Proposal | Inventory / deliverable | Guaranteed volume | Bonus | SYNTHETIC price basis | SYNTHETIC quoted amount |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Publisher A | Premium homepage + section display package | 6,000,000 impressions | 1,000,000 impressions | Rp12,000 CPM on guaranteed impressions | Rp72,000,000 |
| Publisher B | Contextual section-page display package | 4,500,000 impressions | 250,000 impressions | Rp14,000 CPM on guaranteed impressions | Rp63,000,000 |
| Publisher C | One sponsored article with publisher production | Not guaranteed | None | Fixed project fee | Rp18,000,000 |

All prices are **SYNTHETIC, pre-tax**. The exercise does not state the real tax treatment, payable amount or availability of any real publisher. Production is included only in fictional Publisher C's stated package; no other external fee is assumed in this toy model. In an actual quotation, check taxes, production, agency fees, data/measurement, third-party serving, discounts, rebates and other charges separately.

### 3.1 Normalize the display proposals

Formula:

`CPM = (Media cost ÷ billable impressions) × 1,000`

| Calculation | Publisher A | Publisher B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Guaranteed impressions | 6,000,000 | 4,500,000 |
| Guaranteed media cost | Rp72,000,000 | Rp63,000,000 |
| Contracted CPM | Rp12,000 | Rp14,000 |
| Bonus impressions | 1,000,000 | 250,000 |
| Total impressions if bonus is actually delivered | 7,000,000 | 4,750,000 |
| Illustrative effective CPM including delivered bonus | Rp10,285.71 | Rp13,263.16 |

Cross-check:
- Publisher A: Rp72,000,000 ÷ 6,000,000 × 1,000 = Rp12,000 contracted CPM; Rp72,000,000 ÷ 7,000,000 × 1,000 = Rp10,285.71 illustrative effective CPM.
- Publisher B: Rp63,000,000 ÷ 4,500,000 × 1,000 = Rp14,000 contracted CPM; Rp63,000,000 ÷ 4,750,000 × 1,000 = Rp13,263.16 illustrative effective CPM.

**Do not treat bonus impressions as guaranteed paid volume until the booking document defines them.** Confirm placement, dates, audience, reporting, makegood eligibility and whether bonus inventory is genuinely incremental and comparable to paid inventory. A lower effective CPM does not automatically mean higher audience quality or business value.

### 3.2 Budget decision

- A + B = Rp72,000,000 + Rp63,000,000 = **Rp135,000,000** before tax.
- A + B + C = Rp135,000,000 + Rp18,000,000 = **Rp153,000,000** before tax.
- The full three-supplier proposal is **Rp3,000,000 above** the Rp150,000,000 ceiling (2% over the ceiling).

A defensible provisional decision is to keep A + B within the ceiling and hold C pending a revised quote, explicit scope trade-off or additional written budget approval. Do not book the three packages together at the quoted assumptions. A+B have 15 million rupiah of nominal headroom before tax/other unmodelled costs, but this is not automatically spendable contingency.

The combined A+B display-only contracted CPM is Rp135,000,000 ÷ 10,500,000 × 1,000 = **Rp12,857.14**. If the 1,250,000 bonus impressions are actually delivered and are comparable to the paid placements, the illustrative CPM becomes Rp135,000,000 ÷ 11,750,000 × 1,000 = **Rp11,489.36**. These blended figures must not replace separate publisher/placement reporting or hide differences in audience, position, quality or measurement.

Publisher C's fixed-fee sponsored article must not be converted into a CPM unless an agreed, reliable and comparable view/impression guarantee exists. A content-production package and display inventory are not automatically comparable units.

## 4. RFP / RFQ and evaluation exercise

Prepare one common RFP so every supplier responds against the same scope.

### Required RFP fields

1. Advertiser, campaign objective, geography, target definition and exclusions.
2. Flight dates, booking deadlines, material deadlines and availability window.
3. Inventory, placement, format, environment, device, audience and any category exclusivity.
4. Billable unit and definition: impressions, clicks, unique reach, fixed sponsorship, time-based placement or other deliverable.
5. Guaranteed vs non-guaranteed volume; pacing and delivery schedule.
6. Gross price, discount, net price, bonus, production, agency fee, tax, third-party serving and all additional charges as separate lines.
7. Measurement source, methodology, reporting frequency, data access and discrepancies.
8. Creative specs, destination URLs, tracking requirements, brand-safety rules and approval owners.
9. Cancellation, postponement, change control, underdelivery, makegood, credit/refund and dispute procedures.
10. Payment trigger, invoice details, due date, withholding/tax documents and authorized signatories.

### Comparison framework (SYNTHETIC weights for practice only)

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience and contextual fit | 25% | Audience definition, target match, geography, context and exclusions |
| Inventory and placement quality | 20% | Position, format, device, viewability evidence if agreed, adjacency and availability |
| Measurement credibility | 15% | Source, methodology, billable definition, reporting and independent verification |
| Guaranteed delivery and remedy | 15% | Guaranteed volume, schedule, shortfall measurement and remedy |
| Total commercial value | 15% | Net payable, unit comparability, bonus, production and hidden/conditional charges |
| Operational and governance readiness | 10% | Creative requirements, deadlines, owners, approvals, change log, invoice and dispute handling |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

Score only what the proposal actually evidences. Use **Not evidenced** rather than an invented score. The weights are an illustrative training choice, not a validated industry model.

### Required learner output

- RFP/RFQ document;
- three-proposal comparison workbook;
- quote clarification log;
- assumptions/exclusions register;
- recommendation memo with a clear budget decision; and
- approval record for the next step.

## 5. Negotiation plan

Before negotiation, document:

| Item | Example prompt |
|---|---|
| Objective | What commercial, audience or delivery outcome must improve? |
| Target | What price/terms would represent a good outcome? |
| Walk-away limit | What cost, risk or term makes the offer unacceptable? |
| BATNA | What alternative supplier, placement, timing or channel can be used? |
| Leverage | Volume, repeat booking, timing flexibility, creative readiness or competing proposal |
| Priority terms | Net rate, premium placement, guarantees, reporting, category exclusivity, cancellation, makegood, payment and usage rights |
| Concession log | What is offered, what is received in return, who approved it and when? |
| Final record | Written confirmation, quote version, expiry, approver and booking reference |

Do not describe a rate-card discount as a saving without validating the underlying rate, inventory comparability, actual delivery and full payable cost. Value-add should be assessed on audience fit and usable delivery, not only on a nominal rate-card value.

## 6. Booking / IO acceptance checklist

The signed/accepted booking document should be reconciled to the approved proposal and RFP. Use this as a procurement/operational checklist, not a substitute for the publisher's actual form or legal review.

| Control | Required record |
|---|---|
| Parties | Correct advertiser, agency (if applicable), publisher legal entity and authorized contacts |
| Order identity | Booking / IO number, quote version and date |
| Scope | Campaign name, inventory, placements, formats, device/environment and exclusions |
| Delivery | Guaranteed units, bonus units, schedule, pacing, dates and reporting definition |
| Commercials | Currency, gross/net basis, discounts, fixed fees, production, other charges and maximum spend |
| Taxes | Tax treatment and invoice documents confirmed by finance/tax reviewer; never copy an old rate-card statement without current confirmation |
| Creative | Specifications, material deadline, destination URL, approval, rights and version |
| Measurement | Source of truth, billable count, reporting, discrepancy process and data ownership/access |
| Shortfall | Threshold, notification window, calculation, comparable makegood requirements, remedy deadlines and escalation |
| Change / cancellation | Written approval, deadlines, cancellation/postponement cost and inventory availability |
| Payment | Invoice trigger, due date, payment method, bank details verified out-of-band and dispute process |
| Governance | Signatories, precedence among quote/IO/terms/addendum, confidentiality, data protection and legal review |

No money or inventory should be committed based on a draft quotation alone. Require the approved booking confirmation and applicable terms. Any change must be versioned and acknowledged by the relevant authorized parties.

## 7. Delivery and proof-of-performance simulation

**SYNTHETIC delivery outcome:**

| Publisher | Guaranteed impressions | Reported actual impressions | Shortfall | Shortfall rate | Contract CPM | Illustrative shortfall media value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 6,000,000 | 5,280,000 | 720,000 | 12.00% | Rp12,000 | Rp8,640,000 |
| B | 4,500,000 | 4,000,000 | 500,000 | 11.11% | Rp14,000 | Rp7,000,000 |
| **Total** | **10,500,000** | **9,280,000** | **1,220,000** | — | — | **Rp15,640,000** |

Calculation cross-check:
- A shortfall = 6,000,000 − 5,280,000 = 720,000. Illustrative value = 720,000 ÷ 1,000 × Rp12,000 = Rp8,640,000.
- B shortfall = 4,500,000 − 4,000,000 = 500,000. Illustrative value = 500,000 ÷ 1,000 × Rp14,000 = Rp7,000,000.
- Combined shortfall = 720,000 + 500,000 = 1,220,000. Illustrative value = Rp8,640,000 + Rp7,000,000 = Rp15,640,000.

**This is an analytical value estimate, not an automatic refund or credit.** The remedy depends on the signed booking terms and verified delivery. First validate the report and billable definition, then notify the publisher within the contractual window, preserve evidence, request the remedy defined in the booking, and obtain written acceptance of any replacement placement/credit. Comparable audience, context, position, dates and quality must be checked before accepting makegood inventory.

### Evidence pack

- approved brief, RFP and selected proposal;
- signed/accepted IO and all applicable terms;
- creative versions and approval timestamps;
- publisher delivery reports and any agreed third-party report;
- dates, placements, screenshots/URLs and delivery proof;
- discrepancy log and calculation workbook;
- written makegood / credit / remediation agreement; and
- final reconciliation and approver record.

The sample does not prescribe a unilateral invoice deduction or a legal right to withhold payment. Finance/procurement must apply the signed terms, dispute window and applicable tax/invoice rules. Any approved credit note, makegood or adjustment must be traceable to the original IO.

### Variance categories

- Price / quantity mismatch;
- wrong placement or date;
- missing or invalid delivery evidence;
- underdelivery / overdelivery;
- unapproved bonus substitution;
- production / agency / third-party cost omitted or duplicated;
- tax and invoice-document mismatch;
- duplicate line item or wrong billing entity; and
- unresolved dispute / credit / makegood.

## 9. Partner scorecard and investment recommendation

Review the publisher on more than cost:

| Dimension | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Audience quality | Agreed target, source, period and methodology |
| Inventory quality | Placement, context, adjacency and confirmed availability |
| Delivery reliability | Guaranteed vs delivered, pacing and discrepancy |
| Commercial discipline | Quote clarity, changes, hidden charges and invoice accuracy |
| Operational quality | Responsiveness, creative QA, reporting and escalation |
| Governance | Contract compliance, privacy/rights, approvals and documentation |
| Value realization | Useful bonus, verified outcome and repeat-booking rationale |

A supplier may have a competitive CPM but poor delivery or weak audience fit. A premium package may be defensible if its audience, context and guaranteed value are evidenced. Document trade-offs and residual uncertainty rather than forcing a single numeric winner.

## 12. Primary-source notes and limitations

1. **The Jakarta Post — Content Funding Classifications / advertising terms** (publisher page): https://www.thejakartapost.com/content-funding-classifications  
   The page describes reservation through its Advertising Department, a signed Booking Order, insertion information, media-kit/rate-card specifications, invoice timing, payment terms and changes to rate cards. It is a direct publisher example, not an agency-wide buying SOP or universal Indonesian contract. The page currently displays a 10% VAT statement; do **not** copy that figure into this exercise or a real plan without current finance/tax confirmation. The DGT's 3 January 2025 explanation of PMK 131/2024 says non-luxury taxable goods/services use a 12% statutory rate applied to an 11/12 tax base (effective 11% for most such supplies), while luxury taxable goods/services use 12% on the full relevant base. The exact treatment of a particular advertising invoice must be confirmed by the publisher and tax/finance reviewer.
2. **IAB / 4A's Standard Terms and Conditions for Internet Advertising for Media Buys One Year or Less, Version 3.0**: https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IAB_4As-tsandcs-FINAL.pdf  
   This is a US-oriented standard for internet advertising media buys of one year or less. It is a useful comparison for IO fields such as deliverables, prices, maximum spend, dates and ad-server identity, but it is not Indonesian law, a complete direct publisher-buying contract or a substitute for publisher-specific terms and counsel review.
3. **Indonesia Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) — PPN 2025: Kebijakan Baru, Beban Pajak Tetap Ringan untuk Masyarakat**: https://pajak.go.id/id/siaran-pers/ppn-2025-kebijakan-baru-beban-pajak-tetap-ringan-untuk-masyarakat  
   Official explanation dated 3 January 2025 of PMK 131/2024. It supports the tax warning above, but does not replace a current tax review for a specific media transaction.
4. A real publisher rate card is time-, placement-, volume-, audience- and contract-specific. The existence of a public rate card does not prove that its rates are current, available or applicable to a particular buyer. Obtain the current media kit and written quote directly from the relevant media owner.

**Validation boundary:** The pack is a Tier 3 training simulation only. It improves practice coverage for T08 but does not close the primary-source gap for current publisher proposals, agency buying SOPs, valid local contract/IO examples, legal/procurement approval, current tax treatment or 169-chapter objective-level resource sign-off.
