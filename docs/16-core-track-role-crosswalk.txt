## Cross-track decisions that must remain explicit

1. **Performance Marketing Specialist / Performance Strategist:** these titles appear in agency evidence but are not automatically Full-Stack, Paid Social, Paid Search or Media Planner. Map each job by scope: cross-platform execution, strategy, measurement, budget and commercial accountability. Treat as unresolved crosswalk/role orientation until direct evidence is reconciled.
2. **Organic Social Media Specialist:** Community does not cover all organic social planning, publishing, platform-native content, calendar, distribution and social analytics. Interim recommendation: named specialization under Content Strategy & Creation, while Community remains moderation, participation, service and advocacy.
3. **Direct Digital Media Buying vs Programmatic vs Traditional:** keep mechanics distinct. Direct publisher buying belongs in Track 8; DSP buying in Track 6; offline broadcast/print/OOH in Elective 18.
4. **Google Ads Multi-channel:** treat as platform specialization/crosswalk within Paid Search and Full-Stack; separate YouTube/Display/Shopping/Demand Gen from DV360.
5. **Media Analytics vs Marketing Science:** distinguish reporting/diagnosis from causal measurement, experimentation methodology and advanced modelling.
