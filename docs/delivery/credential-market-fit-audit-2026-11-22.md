# Credential Market-Fit Audit — Indonesia and Global Agency Practice

**Audit date:** 22 November 2026  
**Repository:** `putratryarga/putratryarga-dpa`  
**Branch:** `audit/dpa-release-readiness-2026-11-21`  
**Purpose:** Filter learner-facing Earn routes by relevance to Indonesian hiring demand and credible global agency practice. This is not a catalogue of every credential that exists.

## Executive decision

The previous 75-route catalogue was too broad for the DPA's default Indonesia learner journey. It contained awards that were valid in their own markets but weakly supported by Indonesian role demand, were limited to a product/customer account, were simulation or practice completion rather than a professional signal, or were mismatched to the chapter objective.

The current curated registry contains **65 routes** after removing 21 weak-fit or out-of-market routes from the default Earn experience and adding 11 issuer routes with stronger Indonesian-market alignment.

## Decision standard

A credential is eligible for default Earn only when it passes the following tests:

1. **Indonesia-market relevance:** an Indonesian employer, agency, brand, or regional delivery role names the platform, skill, or a directly equivalent workflow; alternatively, it is a recognised industry standard with direct fit to a core job family.
2. **Global agency credibility:** the skill is used in credible agency practice, media operations, client delivery, measurement, CRM/lifecycle, SEO, creator or affiliate work. A globally available badge alone is not enough.
3. **Issuer and award proof:** an official issuer page identifies a real exam, credential, certificate, completion award or badge. A course page without an award stays in Learn.
4. **Chapter fit:** the assessment content supports the actual chapter objective. Platform credentials are not represented as cross-platform competency.
5. **Cost and access transparency:** course access, award issuance, exam fee, retake, renewal, language, prerequisites and Indonesia availability are recorded separately. Unknown is not labelled free.
6. **No artificial credential requirement:** where a role objective has no relevant award, DPA uses its applied deliverable and portfolio evidence instead of inserting an unrelated badge.

The route catalogue is not a substitute for current job-description review. Job postings are volatile and the observed sample below is a dated snapshot.

## Routes retained in default Earn

### Performance media and paid search

- **Google Ads Search, Display, Measurement, Video and Shopping Certifications** — official Skillshop foundational product-area certifications. They are free, assessed, downloadable/shareable and distinct from the separate virtually proctored Google Ads Professional Certifications (Display/Search/Video), which Google says are limited-access and not generally public. Standard Google Ads certification assessment requires 80%, allows 75 minutes, has a 24-hour retake wait and is valid one year; Bahasa Indonesia is supported. Shopping is retained because Indonesian marketplace/e-commerce roles explicitly mention Google Shopping Ads.
- **Microsoft Advertising Search Certification** — retained as a secondary paid-search option because the Publicis Indonesia Paid Search Manager role names Microsoft Ads. Microsoft Display & Video, Retail and Programmatic paths were not retained as default Earn routes because the audited Indonesian JDs did not establish equivalent demand.
- **Google Analytics Certification** — retained for GA/measurement roles; do not assume the exam is free solely because preparation learning is free.
- **DV360 and CM360 Certifications** — retained because Indonesian agency biddable and operations roles name DV360 and CM360/Floodlight.
- **Meta Blueprint and TikTok Academy completion awards** — retained as issuer course/achievement credentials, not relabelled as professional exams.
- **Meta Media Buying / Media Planning / Marketing Science and TikTok Media Buying** — retained as optional professional certifications, separately identified from completion awards and with price/access checks.
- **LinkedIn Advertising Fundamentals and Marketing Measurement** — retained as supplementary B2B/platform routes because agency hiring evidence names LinkedIn Ads as a plus. The routes are English-language and platform-specific; they are not core substitutes for Google/Meta/TikTok.
- **The Trade Desk Edge Academy Certified: Data-Driven Planning** — retained as optional programmatic vendor certification because TTD appears in agency role requirements.
- **IAB DBPC and DAOC** — retained as optional industry certifications for buying/planning and ad operations. Their higher fee means they are not beginner prerequisites.

### SEO and search visibility

- **Semrush SEO Crash Course and Technical SEO & AI Search Essentials** — retained because Indonesian SEO hiring evidence names Semrush.
- **Ahrefs Certification** — added. Issuer evidence states 67 questions, one hour, 75% pass, three attempts, free exam, use of a paid or non-paid Ahrefs account, and a one-year shareable certificate. This validates Ahrefs tool use, not all SEO strategy or technical implementation.
- **HubSpot AEO Fundamentals** — retained as vendor learning certification for AEO fundamentals; it does not prove rankings or AI citations.

### CRM, lifecycle and marketing automation

- **HubSpot Inbound, Email, Digital Marketing, Content and Social Media certifications** — retained where course objectives fit DPA chapters and the public issuer identifies a certificate/assessment. These are vendor course certifications, not broad independent CRM qualifications.
- **MoEngage Growth Marketer — Credential for Completion** — added. The official path covers behavioral segmentation, lifecycle automation, email, push, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app messaging, flows and optimisation. The path page does not state an Indonesia-specific price/access condition; account-level completion must be checked.
- **MoEngage Analytics Expert — Credential for Completion** — added. The official path covers event-based behavioral analytics, funnels, cohorts, retention and path analysis. This is a MoEngage-specific completion credential, not a neutral analytics qualification.
- **Braze Certified Digital Strategist** — added as an optional vendor professional certification. Braze states that the certification validates campaign strategy, technical-stack understanding and lifecycle journeys; its public pages do not disclose the current exam fee. Do not advertise it as free.
- **Salesforce Certified Marketing Cloud Engagement Foundations** — added as a paid entry-level route. The current guide lists 40 multiple-choice questions, 70 minutes, 65% pass and USD 75 plus applicable tax; no prerequisite; free retake.
- **Salesforce Certified Marketing Cloud Engagement Specialist** — added as an advanced optional route. Salesforce's current pricing page lists USD 200 plus tax and USD 100 retake. It maps to email, subscriber/data management and inbox delivery.

### Programmatic, creator and affiliate

- **Meltwater Influencer Marketing Certification I and II** — retained as issuer industry certifications. Certification II adds influencer selection and conversion tactics; both are listed as free by Meltwater Academy. Product-specific Klear learning is not elevated to default Earn.
- **Meta creator/Partnership Ads and relevant TikTok Academy creative achievements** — retained as platform-specific supplemental awards, not proof of creator contracting, negotiation, disclosure compliance or live campaign success.
- **impact.com Affiliate Marketing & Partnerships Industry Certification** — retained for Track 15 because the issuer identifies an industry certification and its competencies align with affiliate program strategy, partner recruitment, commercial terms, tracking and performance. Request-access approval and Indonesian account eligibility still need testing.

### Platforms not added merely because an award exists

Pinterest Academy, Snap Focus and Adobe credentials were not added to default Earn in this audit because the reviewed Indonesia agency/job evidence did not establish sufficiently direct demand for those issuer-specific routes. This is a market-fit decision, not a claim that those providers do not issue awards. Revisit only with a concrete Indonesian employer, agency or client-role evidence trail.

### Marketplace credential gap — deliberately not fabricated

The Indonesian role sample strongly supports Shopee Ads, TikTok Shop Ads and Lazada Sponsored Solutions. However, this audit did not verify a public, current, issuer-awarded Shopee Ads or Tokopedia Ads professional certificate with clear assessment and issuance conditions. The learner path should therefore teach those systems and require a practical marketplace campaign artifact, but must not claim an unverified certificate. TikTok Academy ad Achievements are not automatically a TikTok Shop seller certificate.

## Cost, access and learner-facing rules

- A free learning page does not prove that the final exam or award is free.
- A completion credential is shown as a completion award, not a professional certification.
- A tool-vendor credential (Ahrefs, MoEngage, Braze, HubSpot, Salesforce Marketing Cloud) must state its product-specific scope.
- The Salesforce Foundations and Specialist exams are paid; local checkout currency/tax must be checked.
- Braze exam fee is not stated in the public material checked; do not label it free.
- MoEngage public course pages identify Credential for Completion but do not settle local fees, language or account-level access.
- LinkedIn credentials are English-language in the current issuer help listing; do not imply Bahasa Indonesia exam delivery.
- TikTok Academy learning is free according to TikTok's Indonesia page. The separate TikTok Media Buying Certification is a proctored professional exam and not the same award.
- No external credential is made mandatory for DPA chapter completion. Chapter deliverables and portfolio evidence remain primary.
