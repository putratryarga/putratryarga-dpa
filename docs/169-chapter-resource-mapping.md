# 169-Chapter Resource Mapping Register

**Date:** 4 November 2026  
**Scope:** All 169 core chapters across 16 tracks.  
**Purpose:** Provide a traceable official learning/reference source and a chapter-specific applied practice pointer for every chapter. The resources are shared at track level where the same platform or standard spans multiple chapters; the applied deliverable is taken from each chapter's own practice prompt.

## Mapping logic

- **Tier 1:** Official issuer/platform learning route relevant to the role family.
- **Tier 2:** Official platform documentation, industry standards or a second issuer source that supports the technical method.
- **Tier 3:** The chapter's own practice/deliverable from the current guide; this is the evidence the learner must produce, not another course.
- A link's existence does not prove account access, regional availability, current price, or credential eligibility. Those are recorded separately in `resource-credential-validation.md`.
- A platform learning path is not automatically a substitute for independent standards or hands-on build evidence.
- The mapping is a complete first-pass coverage map; final chapter-by-chapter SME confirmation of source-to-objective fit remains open.

## Source-type and credential controls

- **Google Skillshop** provides free online product training. Google Ads certification is a separate product accreditation with its own pass score, duration and validity rules (see the issuer register).
- **StackAdapt Programmatic Masterclass** is vendor-issued, free, 8 modules / 120 minutes and advertises a certificate; it is not an independent industry accreditation.
- **The Trade Desk Programmatic 101** is a one-hour introductory course; no certificate/badge is claimed in this mapping unless the learner's account confirms one.
- **IAB Tech Lab** pages are standards/reference material, not credentials.
- **Walmart Connect Academy** and **Amazon Ads Academy** credentials are platform/vendor-specific; regional access, exam price and validity must be checked at enrollment.
- **HubSpot Academy** advertises free online courses and certifications; course-specific completion rules apply.

- **impact.com Partnerships Experience Academy** advertises free affiliate/partnership certification learning paths; these are vendor/industry-provider credentials, not platform-neutral accreditation.
- **TikTok One Creator Marketplace** is a platform workflow/reference, not a credential. Eligibility differs by region.
