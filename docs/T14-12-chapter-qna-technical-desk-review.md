## Local and platform boundaries

1. **Indonesia advertising ethics:** EPI 2020 is an industry ethical code covering advertising messages and advertising actors in Indonesia; it is not itself a statute or a substitute for legal advice. [Dewan Periklanan Indonesia — EPI 2020](https://dewaniklanindonesia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/EPI-2020.pdf).
2. **Consumer claims:** UU 8/1999 on Consumer Protection is recorded as in force in BPK's legal database. Product claims and testimonials must be truthful, supportable and reviewed for the relevant category. [BPK — UU 8/1999](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no8-tahun-1999).
3. **Personal data:** UU 27/2022 on Personal Data Protection is recorded as in force. Creator addresses, audience insights, contact lists and other identifiable data require purpose, lawful basis, minimization, security and retention review appropriate to the controller/processor roles. [BPK — UU 27/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-).
4. **Copyright and content rights:** UU 28/2014 on Copyright is recorded as in force. The agreement must separately define content license/assignment, portrait/likeness, music, raw footage, editing, derivative works, territory, media, duration and paid usage. [BPK — UU 28/2014](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38690/uu-no-17-tahun-2014).
5. **Meta:** branded content rules require partner tagging when value is exchanged; partnership ads require creator authorization. Platform disclosure and ad authorization are separate from a broader contract licence. [Meta creator marketplace and partnership ads](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/).
6. **TikTok:** branded content disclosure is available in the creator workflow; Spark Ads can use organic posts from creators only with authorization. Authorization duration and status must be checked for the specific asset before paid activation. [TikTok branded content disclosure](https://support.tiktok.com/id/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service?lang=en), [Spark Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/spark-ads?Sid=1).
7. **YouTube:** paid product placement, sponsorships and endorsements must be declared through the paid promotion setting; creators and brands remain responsible for local legal disclosure duties. [YouTube paid-promotion disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=id).

## Critical distinctions retained

- KOL/KOC and creator tiers are practice labels, not universally standardized legal or platform categories.
- Follower growth, comments and engagement rate are screening signals, not standalone proof of fraud or authentic influence.
- A gifted product is not automatically a contracted post; barter deliverables and no-obligation seeding must be distinguished.
- Organic posting permission is not automatically paid usage permission. Rights scope must cover identity, content, edits, channel, geography, term and authorization.
- Platform ad authorization is not the same as copyright/likeness licence or a campaign contract.
- Paid amplification must be separated from organic performance. Total views cannot automatically be claimed as organic reach.
- Discount codes and attributed conversions do not by themselves estimate incremental causal impact.
- EPI and platform policies are not substitutes for a qualified legal review of category-specific claims, data collection, rights and disclosures.
