## Local and platform boundaries

1. **Indonesia advertising ethics:** EPI 2020 is an industry self-regulatory code, not a statute. Clause 1.16 says consumer testimony must reflect real experience and not be exaggerated; clause 1.17 says endorsement is by an individual, not an institution or group. This distinction matters when drafting testimonial, influencer and endorsement briefs. [Dewan Periklanan Indonesia — EPI 2020](https://dewaniklanindonesia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/EPI-2020.pdf).
2. **Consumer claims:** UU 8/1999 on Consumer Protection is recorded as in force in BPK's legal database. Product claims and testimonials must be truthful, supportable and reviewed for the relevant category. [BPK — UU 8/1999](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no8-tahun-1999).
3. **Personal data:** UU 27/2022 on Personal Data Protection is recorded as in force. Creator addresses, audience insights, contact lists and other identifiable data require purpose, lawful basis, minimization, security and retention review appropriate to the controller/processor roles. [BPK — UU 27/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-).
4. **Copyright and content rights:** UU 28/2014 on Copyright is recorded as in force. The agreement must separately define content license/assignment, portrait/likeness, music, raw footage, editing, derivative works, territory, media, duration and paid usage. [BPK — UU 28/2014](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38690/uu-no-28-tahun-2014).
5. **Meta:** Meta's official Creator Marketplace explanation describes creator-brand partnerships and the ability to amplify eligible organic Instagram content as Partnership Ads, with the Paid Partnership label available for branded content. Platform partnership/advertising authorization remains distinct from copyright, likeness and broader contract licences. [Meta Creator Marketplace and Partnership Ads](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/), [Instagram branded-content Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/116947042301556).
6. **TikTok:** creators must enable Commercial Content Disclosure for content promoting a brand, product or service; platform labels differ by the relationship. Spark Ads can use a creator's organic post only with the relevant asset authorization. Check the specific post's authorization state and expiry before activation. [TikTok creator disclosure](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators?lang=id), [Spark Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/spark-ads?Sid=1).
7. **YouTube:** product placements, sponsorships, endorsements and other commercial relationships must be declared using YouTube Studio's paid-promotion setting. The platform label does not discharge separate local legal, consumer-protection or contractual disclosure obligations. [YouTube paid-promotion disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=id).

## Critical distinctions retained

- KOL/KOC and creator tiers are practice labels, not universally standardized legal or platform categories.
- Follower growth, comments and engagement rate are screening signals, not standalone proof of fraud or authentic influence.
- A gifted product is not automatically a contracted post; barter deliverables and no-obligation seeding must be distinguished.
- Organic posting permission is not automatically paid usage permission. Rights scope must cover identity, content, edits, channel, geography, term and authorization.
- Platform ad authorization is not the same as copyright/likeness licence or a campaign contract.
- Paid amplification must be separated from organic performance. Total views cannot automatically be claimed as organic reach.
- Discount codes and attributed conversions do not by themselves estimate incremental causal impact.
- EPI and platform policies are not substitutes for a qualified legal review of category-specific claims, data collection, rights and disclosures.

## Regulatory-currentness follow-up — 5 November 2026

The source-fit review adds Permendag 19/2026 (effective 8 June 2026; repeals Permendag 31/2023) as a current PMSE/electronic-advertising reference for applicable T14 objectives. Articles 31–32 concern responsibility for electronic-ad material, non-misleading information, and consumer reviews/testimonials in electronic advertising. Apply the regulation only within its scope and obtain qualified Indonesian legal review; this is not a legal opinion.

The EPI 2020 distinction is clarified: clause 1.16 concerns consumer testimony, including genuine personal experience, no exaggeration and signed written support; clause 1.17 concerns individual endorsement. EPI is an industry self-regulatory code, not legislation.
