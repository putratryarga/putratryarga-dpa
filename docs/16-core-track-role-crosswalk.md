## Taxonomy decisions (closed for curriculum architecture)

1. **Full-Stack vs Performance Marketing:** Track 1 is an integrated generalist learning path. Performance Marketing is a cross-channel paid-growth job family/pathway. Map actual job descriptions by scope; do not equate the two or imply universal expert execution.
2. **Organic Social vs Content vs Community:** Organic Social remains a distinct market job family and a named specialization under Track 13 to preserve the 16-track constraint. Track 13 owns content/editorial strategy and organic channel publishing. Track 16 owns community relationships, moderation, participation and advocacy. Track 5 owns paid social activation.
3. **Media Analytics vs Marketing Science:** Track 10 contains two explicit pathways. Media Analytics covers data quality, reporting and diagnosis; Marketing Science adds experimentation, causal measurement, incrementality, MMM/econometrics and advanced modelling.
4. **Paid Search vs Google Ads multi-channel:** Track 4 is search-specialist. Google Ads is a platform suite. Separate Search from YouTube, Display, Shopping, Demand Gen and other platform-specific campaigns. SA360 is search management; DV360 is a DSP.
5. **Media Investment vs Programmatic vs Traditional:** Track 8 is direct publisher/media-owner commercial buying; Track 6 is automated DSP/RTB trading; Elective 18 is offline TV/radio/print/OOH/DOOH/cinema/sponsorship planning and buying.

These decisions close the taxonomy design question, not the empirical evidence audit. If new direct job descriptions materially contradict a boundary, record the JD, geography, date, scope and proposed change before revising taxonomy.

## Evidence interpretation and known limitations

- Use evidence classes A (direct employer JD), B (adjacent role), C (agency capability), D (employer career-index/title or third-party/historical listing), E (industry/vendor standard), G (direct evidence gap).
- A careers-index title proves a role family/title exists, not the full competency depth.
- Current evidence is uneven across the five holding groups. No claim of complete 5×16 JD validation is made.
- Organic Social is preserved as a specialization because the source research identifies distinct strategy, publishing, listening and community functions; the final 16-track constraint is respected without collapsing it into Community.
- Refer to the consolidated Source of Truth for detailed role competency matrices, evidence coverage, source register, chapter audit schema and release gates.
