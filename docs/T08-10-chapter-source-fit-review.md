## 1. Why this batch was necessary

The prior T08 rows repeated the Google Marketing Platform support hub and IAB Tech Lab general-solutions hub across nearly every chapter. Those broad hubs were weak evidence for the commercial objectives on publisher RFPs, inventory assessment, negotiation, booking, proof of delivery, invoice reconciliation and investment review.

This batch adds specific public evidence, especially the **official Bisnis Indonesia Rate Card 2026** and the separate **Bisnis.com Regional Rate Card 2026**. The documents show that Media Order requirements, lead times, cancellation fees, placement types and proof-retention policies are publisher- and product-specific. They are not a universal Indonesian standard or a cross-publisher benchmark.

### Specific evidence checked

- The Bisnis Indonesia 2026 PDF identifies itself as a 2026 rate card and publishes inventory/format price units. Its Media Order section requires an order signed and officially stamped, sets deadlines by placement, and requires written cancellation. It states late-cancellation charges for specified order types. The same document says proof-ad storage is limited to one month and the publisher is not obliged to deliver proof to the advertiser. Its tariff section says prices are in Rupiah and exclude PPN 11%. These are statements from this publisher's own card only.
- The Bisnis.com Regional 2026 card separately requires a signed Media Order, specifies receipt at least five working days before publication, and sets a 50% late-cancellation charge. It states rates are in Rupiah and exclude PPN 11%. Do not combine these terms with Harian Bisnis Indonesia.
- Google Ad Manager distinguishes direct-sold and indirect-sold transactions. A traditional direct-sold transaction can be negotiated externally, then entered into orders/line items; billing/payment/reconciliation are manually handled by the publisher. Programmatic Direct is a distinct product workflow.
- MRC material concerns methodology, rating research, viewability, IVT, reach or attention. It does not establish commercial remedies for underdelivery. Makegood, replacement placement, credit or refund depends on the signed booking.
- ANA describes its June 2023 template as a customized starting point for U.S. advertiser/media-agency relationships. It is not a local publisher booking form.
- The Jakarta Post page's 10% VAT text is dated. Keep it only as a historical publisher example. The primary legal references for transaction-level invoice checks are PMK 131/2024 and DJP PER-1/PJ/2025. Do not hard-code a universal VAT rate into the exercise; classify the actual transaction with finance/tax review.

## 4. Required teaching controls

1. **Do not present a single rate card as an Indonesian market benchmark.** A public rate card is a publisher's list price for specific inventory/date; the real quote varies by availability, audience, placement, flight, volume, discount, production, fees and negotiated terms.
2. **Do not mix the two 2026 Bisnis documents.** Harian Bisnis Indonesia and Bisnis.com Regional are separate products with different booking/cancellation terms.
3. **Do not treat print rate units as digital CPM or CPP.** Normalize denominator, audience, placement, period, format, guarantee and billable definition first.
4. **Separate proof of inventory from audience verification.** A screenshot/proof-of-publication, publisher report, ad-server impression, viewability result, verified audience and incremental outcome are different constructs.
5. **Separate a Media Order from a complete legal contract.** Check quote, signed terms, scope, cancellation, rights, privacy, tax, dispute and approval; legal/procurement owns the legal decision.
6. **No unilateral deduction or automatic makegood assumption.** Remedy follows the signed agreement, evidence and notice window.
7. **Do not hard-code tax based on a publisher's page.** Verify supplier/customer status, service category, DPP, tax-invoice requirements and current rules with finance/tax.
8. **Keep all simulation data labelled synthetic.** Fictional publishers, volumes, rates and Rp150 million budget in `docs/T08-direct-publisher-buying-simulation.md` are training assumptions, not public quotes or actual results.
